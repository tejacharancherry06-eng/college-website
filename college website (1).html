About Our College

8+ Years of Academic Excellence

Established with a vision to provide quality education, Anam Sanjeeva Reddy Degree College has been nurturing young minds for over eight years. Located in a serene environment, our campus fosters holistic development and academic excellence.

We offer state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and a vibrant campus life that prepares students for global challenges.

  • Experienced and dedicated faculty
  • Modern laboratories and classrooms
  • Holistic, student-centred campus life
